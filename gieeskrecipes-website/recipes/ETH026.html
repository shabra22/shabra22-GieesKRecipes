🍲 Ethiopian Spicy Beef Stew

ቀይ ወጥ — Key Wat (Beef)

Ethiopian Key Wat Beef — the everyday spicy beef wat. Onions dry-caramelised, kibbeh and berbere cooked together, beef slow-simmered for 90 minutes in a deeply red, deeply spiced sauce. The base recipe for all Ethiopian red wats — the building block of the cuisine.

Key Wat (literally 'red stew') is the foundational preparation of Ethiopian cooking — once you understand the dry-onion, kibbeh, berbere sequence, you can make any Ethiopian wat. This is the everyday beef version, without the egg of the celebration version (ETH021).

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 700g beef chuck or stewing beef — cubed
  • 3 large onions — very finely diced
  • 3 tbsp kibbeh
  • 4 tbsp berbere
  • 5 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup water
  • 1.5 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions in a heavy pot with no oil — 20 minutes, stirring often.
  2. Add kibbeh. Cook 2 minutes.
  3. Add berbere. Toast in the kibbeh 3 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, and tomato paste. Cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add beef. Stir to coat.
  6. Add water. Simmer covered 80—90 minutes on very low heat.
  7. The sauce should be thick and dark red. Season.

Chef Tips

  • See ETH021 for the full technique and tips.
  • The quantity of berbere seems excessive until you taste the result.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding oil with onions.
  • Short cooking time.

Cultural Note

Key Wat is the red that defines Ethiopian cuisine — the colour of berbere on injera, the colour of the Ethiopian national dish, the colour that Ethiopians see when they close their eyes and think of food.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 5 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Gently.

Serve with: Injera (ETH001), With Tikel Gomen (ETH086) and Misir Wat (ETH081) for a complete platter
