🍌 Ethiopian Honey Banana Dessert

ሙዝ ዴዘርት — Muz Dezert

Ethiopian Honey Banana — ripe bananas halved and pan-fried in a little oil until golden, then drizzled with Ethiopian honey, cardamom, and a squeeze of lemon. The simplest and most beloved Ethiopian sweet — three ingredients, ten minutes, completely satisfying.

Ethiopian honey banana is the dessert of abundance — when bananas are ripe and honey is available, this combination requires almost no cooking and produces an extraordinary result. The bananas caramelise in the pan, the honey deepens their sweetness, and the cardamom adds complexity.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide pan

  • 4 ripe bananas — peeled, halved lengthwise
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
  • 3 tbsp Ethiopian honey
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Optional: pinch of cinnamon, chopped nuts

    Method

    1. Heat oil or butter in a wide pan over medium heat.
    2. Place banana halves cut-side down.
    3. Cook 3—4 minutes until golden and caramelised on the cut side.
    4. Turn gently.
    5. Drizzle honey over bananas.
    6. Sprinkle cardamom.
    7. Cook 2 more minutes.
    8. Squeeze lemon juice over.
    9. Serve immediately.

    Chef Tips

    • Very ripe bananas — the natural sugars are essential for caramelisation.
    • Add honey at the end — earlier and it burns.
    • Serve immediately — the caramelised bananas deflate quickly.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Underripe bananas.
    • Adding honey too early.

    The Science

    The cut surface of the banana undergoes Maillard reaction and caramelisation simultaneously — the amino acids in the banana flesh react with the natural sugars at the pan surface temperature, producing hundreds of complex flavour compounds. The honey added at the end caramelises rapidly due to its high fructose content.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian honey banana is the dessert that needs no occasion — made when bananas are at their most ripe and eaten with the simple joy of good ingredients combined with minimal intervention. It is the philosophy of Ethiopian cooking in its simplest form.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Best eaten immediately.

    Reheating: Not recommended.

    Serve with: As a simple dessert, With cream or yogurt, Over pancakes (ETH181)
