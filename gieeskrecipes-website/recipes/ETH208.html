🍬 Ethiopian Tamarind Sweet

ተምር ጠጅ — Tamar Tej

Ethiopian Tamarind Sweet — tamarind paste cooked with honey and cardamom until thick, rolled in sesame seeds. The tart-sweet confection of Ethiopia's tamarind-growing regions — intensely flavoured, with the same sweet-sour-spice balance that characterises the best Ethiopian food.

Tamarind grows in Ethiopia's lowland and Rift Valley areas, and the tamarind sweet is the traditional confection of those regions — the intense tartness of tamarind balanced with Ethiopian honey and cardamom produces a flavour unlike any other.

Ingredients

Equipment: Small saucepan

  • 200g tamarind paste
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom

Rolling: sesame seeds or desiccated coconut

    Method

    1. Combine tamarind, honey, sugar, and cardamom in a saucepan.
    2. Cook over medium heat, stirring, 12—15 minutes until very thick.
    3. Test: a drop cooled on a plate should not spread.
    4. Cool 15 minutes until handleable.
    5. Roll into small balls.
    6. Roll in sesame seeds or coconut.
    7. Refrigerate 30 minutes to firm.

    Chef Tips

    • Very thick — it should barely pour when done.
    • Cool slightly before rolling — too hot is impossible to handle.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Too thin — sticky balls that don't hold shape.

    The Science

    Tamarind's tartaric acid (the dominant organic acid) reacts with the honey's sucrose during cooking — producing a mixture that sets differently from pure honey candy, with a softer, more chewy texture due to the acid's effect on sugar crystallisation.

    Cultural Note

    Ethiopian tamarind sweet is the tart-sweet confection that captures the essential balance of Ethiopian food philosophy — in a cuisine that masters the combination of heat, sourness, and richness, even the desserts carry this complexity.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Refrigerate 2 weeks. Room temperature 5 days.

    Reheating: Not applicable.

    Serve with: As a market sweet, At celebrations in lowland communities, With coffee
