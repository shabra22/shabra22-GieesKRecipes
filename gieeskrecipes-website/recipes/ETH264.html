☕ Jimma Coffee and Honey Beef

ጅማ ቡና ሥጋ — Jimma Buna Siga

Jimma Coffee-Braised Beef — beef slow-braised with brewed Jimma coffee, honey, berbere, and onion. The Jimma cooking innovation that uses coffee as a braising liquid — the coffee provides depth, acidity, and the distinctive Jimma coffee character.

Jimma, the great coffee trading city, has developed a cooking tradition that incorporates coffee as a cooking liquid — the braising of beef in strong coffee is a uniquely Jimma culinary innovation.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pot

  • 600g beef — cubed
  • 1 cup strong brewed Jimma coffee
  • 2 onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tbsp berbere
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Dry caramelise onions 18 minutes.
  2. Add kibbeh and berbere. Fry 3 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger.
  4. Add beef. Stir to coat.
  5. Add brewed coffee and honey.
  6. Simmer covered 45 minutes.
  7. The coffee evaporates into the sauce — rich, deep, complex.
  8. Season.

Chef Tips

  • Strong coffee is essential — weak coffee provides nothing.
  • The coffee reduces into the sauce creating an extraordinary depth.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Weak coffee — no flavour impact.

The Science

Coffee contains chlorogenic acids, melanoidins, and hundreds of aromatic compounds — when used as a braising liquid, these compounds concentrate into the sauce. The chlorogenic acids act as natural tenderisers (mild acidity), while the melanoidins add deep brown colour.

Cultural Note

Jimma coffee-braised beef is the culinary statement of a city that has traded coffee for centuries — the ingredient that Jimma is most famous for, applied to its most valued protein. The result is uniquely Jimma.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days.

Reheating: Gently.
