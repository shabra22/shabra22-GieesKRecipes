🥩 Modern Ethiopian Tibs Bowl

ጥብስ ሳህን — Tibs Sahin

Modern Ethiopian Tibs Bowl — berbere-seared beef tibs over teff injera pieces, topped with ayib, silsi, pickled jalapeños, and fresh herbs. The contemporary Ethiopian bowl format — deconstructed traditional tibs for the modern restaurant table.

The tibs bowl is the modern Ethiopian restaurant's response to the bowl food trend — traditional tibs deconstructed and assembled in the global bowl format but using entirely Ethiopian ingredients.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy pan, Serving bowl

  • 400g beef — cubed
  • 2 tbsp kibbeh
  • 1 tsp berbere
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 jalapeños
  • 2 tomatoes

Bowl Build

  • 1 cup torn day-old injera pieces
  • 3 tbsp ayib
  • 3 tbsp silsi
  • Pickled jalapeños
  • Fresh coriander

Method

  1. Make tibs: sear beef in kibbeh with berbere, onion, garlic, jalapeño, tomato. Season.
  2. Layer torn injera in a bowl.
  3. Top with hot tibs.
  4. Add ayib mound on one side.
  5. Spoon silsi on the other.
  6. Scatter pickled jalapeños and coriander.

Chef Tips

  • The ayib and silsi are not mixed — guests combine as they eat.
  • Hot tibs on torn injera softens it — this is intentional.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Mixing everything before serving — presentation is the point.

Cultural Note

The tibs bowl is Addis Ababa's response to the global bowl food trend — the same tibs that Ethiopians have eaten for centuries, presented in a format that connects with a generation that grew up eating both injera and global food. It is tradition wearing contemporary clothing.
