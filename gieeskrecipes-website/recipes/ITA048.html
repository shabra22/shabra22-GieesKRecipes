🥟 Tortellini Alfredo

Tortellini al Burro e Parmigiano

Tortellini Alfredo takes Bologna's classic filled pasta out of its traditional broth and tosses it instead in a rich, silky butter-and-Parmigiano sauce, for a more indulgent alternative presentation.

While purists in Emilia-Romagna serve tortellini exclusively in brodo, tossing them in a buttery Parmigiano sauce has become a widely loved alternative, especially outside the region, letting the delicate meat filling shine against a rich, creamy backdrop.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot, Large skillet, Tongs

  • 1 batch fresh tortellini (as in ITA034, using the same filling and shaping method)
  • 100 g (½ cup) unsalted butter
  • 100 g (1¼ cups) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more for serving
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil and cook the tortellini for 3–4 minutes until they float and the pasta is tender.
  2. Reserve at least 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
  3. In a large skillet, melt the butter over low heat.
  4. Add the drained tortellini directly to the skillet along with a splash of pasta water, tossing gently to coat without breaking the delicate pasta.
  5. Off the heat, gradually add the Parmigiano, tossing continuously and adding pasta water as needed to build a smooth, glossy sauce.
  6. Season generously with black pepper.
  7. Serve immediately, garnished with parsley and extra Parmigiano.

Chef Tips

  • Toss gently rather than vigorously to avoid breaking the delicate, filled pasta.
  • Room-temperature butter emulsifies more smoothly than cold butter.
  • Fresh tortellini gives the best result, but good-quality store-bought works well too if short on time.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Tossing too roughly, causing the tortellini to split open and lose their filling.
  • Overcooking the tortellini, making the delicate pasta wrapper mushy.
  • Adding the cheese over too much heat, causing the sauce to break rather than emulsify.

Cultural Note

While tortellini in brodo remains the most traditional preparation in Bologna, tossing tortellini in butter and cheese has become a beloved variation enjoyed throughout Italy and internationally.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately — the sauce does not hold or reheat well.

Reheating: Not recommended; make fresh for the best texture.

Serve with: A crisp white wine, A simple green salad, Crusty bread
