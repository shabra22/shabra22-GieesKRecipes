🍪 Mostaccioli Calabresi

Mostaccioli

Dense, spiced honey cookies from Calabria, shaped into decorative diamonds or figures and finished with a chocolate or sugar glaze — a traditional feature of Christmas and wedding tables.

Mostaccioli's spice blend, often including cinnamon, cloves, and citrus, along with a generous amount of honey, gives these cookies a dense, deeply flavored character that keeps exceptionally well, making them ideal for the extended holiday season.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Cookie cutters, Baking sheet

  • 400 g (3 cups) 00 flour
  • 150 g (½ cup) honey
  • 80 g (¼ cup) granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 5 g (1 tsp) baking soda
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) warm water
  • 80 g (⅓ cup) unsalted butter, softened (or olive oil for a dairy-free version)
  • 100 g (3.5 oz) dark chocolate, melted, for glazing

Method

  1. Warm the honey gently until fluid, then combine with the sugar, cinnamon, cloves, and orange zest.
  2. Dissolve the baking soda in the warm water and add to the honey mixture.
  3. Add the butter or oil and gradually work in the flour to form a firm, smooth dough.
  4. Rest the dough, covered, for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll out to about 1cm thickness and cut into diamond shapes or other traditional figures.
  6. Arrange on a lined baking sheet and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 15–18 minutes until firm and lightly golden.
  7. Cool completely, then dip the tops in melted chocolate, letting the excess drip off.
  8. Let the chocolate set completely before serving or storing.

Chef Tips

  • Warming the honey first makes it much easier to incorporate smoothly into the dough.
  • These cookies are meant to be dense and firm rather than light and airy — that texture is traditional.
  • They keep exceptionally well and often taste even better a few days after baking, once the spices have settled in.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rolling the dough too thin, losing the characteristic dense, chewy bite.
  • Rushing the chocolate glaze, resulting in an uneven coating.
  • Overbaking, which can make the cookies too hard rather than pleasantly dense.

Health Benefits

  • Honey provides natural sweetness with some antioxidant compounds compared to refined sugar alone.

Cultural Note

Mostaccioli have deep roots across southern Italy, particularly in Calabria, where they remain a fixture of Christmas baking and traditional wedding celebrations.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 weeks.

Reheating: Not applicable.

Serve with: Espresso, A glass of sweet wine
