Pitta 'Nchiusa

A striking spiral-shaped sweet bread from the Calabrian coast, its yeasted dough tightly coiled around a filling of walnuts, raisins, and honey, traditionally baked for Christmas.

Pitta 'nchiusa, meaning 'closed pitta,' is rolled thin, spread with its nut-and-honey filling, then wound into a tight spiral before baking, resulting in a strikingly beautiful cross-section when sliced that has made it a treasured centerpiece of the Calabrian holiday table.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowl, Rolling pin, Round baking pan

  • 500 g (4 cups) 00 flour
  • 7 g (2¼ tsp) active dry yeast
  • 250 ml (1 cup) warm water
  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) extra virgin olive oil
  • 8 g (1½ tsp) salt
  • 200 g (1¾ cups) walnuts, chopped
  • 150 g (1 cup) raisins, soaked and chopped
  • 150 g (½ cup) honey
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • Zest of 1 orange

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and let sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Combine the flour, olive oil, salt, and yeast mixture into a smooth, elastic dough, kneading for 10 minutes.
  3. Let rise, covered, in a warm place for about 1.5–2 hours, until doubled.
  4. Combine the walnuts, drained raisins, honey, cinnamon, and orange zest into a thick, spreadable filling.
  5. Roll the risen dough out into a large, thin rectangle.
  6. Spread the filling evenly over the dough, leaving a small border.
  7. Roll the dough up tightly from one long edge, forming a long log.
  8. Coil the log into a tight spiral and place in a round baking pan.
  9. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 30–35 minutes until deeply golden.
  10. Cool before slicing to reveal the spiral pattern.

Chef Tips

  • Roll the dough as thin and evenly as possible for the most attractive spiral when sliced.
  • Roll the filled log tightly, which helps the finished bread hold its spiral shape neatly.
  • This bread genuinely improves after a day's rest, as the filling flavors settle into the dough.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rolling the dough too thick, resulting in a less defined spiral pattern once sliced.
  • Making the filling too wet, which can cause it to leak out during baking.
  • Rushing the initial rise, resulting in a denser, less airy final bread.

Cultural Note

Pitta 'nchiusa is a treasured Christmas tradition along the Calabrian coast, particularly around Reggio Calabria, its striking spiral cross-section a centerpiece of the holiday dessert table.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 5 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served at room temperature.

Serve with: Espresso
