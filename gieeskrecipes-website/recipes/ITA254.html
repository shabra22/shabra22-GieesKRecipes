🍰 Polenta e Osei

Polenta e Osei

A playful sponge cake shaped and colored to resemble a mound of golden polenta topped with small chocolate 'birds,' a whimsical Bergamo dessert alluding to a once-common regional dish.

This dessert is a lighthearted culinary joke, its yellow sponge base and dusting of cocoa-and-sugar 'crumbs' visually mimicking soft polenta, while marzipan or chocolate shapes formed to look like small roasted birds reference a dish once eaten in the region, now recreated purely for fun.

Ingredients

Equipment: Round cake pan, Electric mixer

  • 4 eggs
  • 120 g (2/3 cup) granulated sugar
  • 100 g (3/4 cup) 00 flour
  • 40 g (1/3 cup) cornstarch
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 150 g (2/3 cup) mascarpone or pastry cream
  • 100 g (3.5 oz) marzipan, tinted yellow
  • 80 g (2.8 oz) dark chocolate, for the birds
  • Powdered sugar and cocoa powder, for dusting

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs and sugar together until pale, thick, and tripled in volume.
  2. Gently fold in the flour, cornstarch, and lemon zest, being careful not to deflate the batter.
  3. Pour into a buttered and floured round cake pan.
  4. Bake at 180C (350F) for 20-25 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool completely, then split the cake and fill with mascarpone or pastry cream.
  6. Roll the tinted yellow marzipan thin and drape over the assembled cake, smoothing to mimic the surface of soft polenta.
  7. Melt the dark chocolate and shape small bird decorations, either freehand or using a small mold.
  8. Arrange the chocolate birds over the marzipan-covered cake.
  9. Dust lightly with powdered sugar and a touch of cocoa to complete the polenta illusion.

Chef Tips

  • Whipping the eggs and sugar to full volume before folding in the dry ingredients is essential for a light sponge.
  • Tinting the marzipan a warm yellow is what completes the visual illusion of polenta.
  • This is a fun, whimsical dessert - do not worry about the birds looking perfectly uniform.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Deflating the whipped egg mixture by folding too roughly, resulting in a denser sponge.
  • Rolling the marzipan too thick, which can overwhelm the delicate sponge underneath.
  • Overbaking the sponge, which dries it out and makes it harder to split evenly.

Cultural Note

Polenta e osei is a whimsical specialty of Bergamo, its visual pun referencing a once-common regional dish of small roasted birds served over polenta, now reimagined purely as a playful dessert.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate, covered, for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Not applicable; served chilled or at room temperature.

Serve with: Espresso
