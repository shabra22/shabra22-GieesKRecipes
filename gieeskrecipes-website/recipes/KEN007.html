🍅 Kenyan Kachumbari

Kachumbari

Kachumbari is Kenya's most vibrant and refreshing side dish — ripe tomatoes, thinly sliced red onions, fresh coriander, chilli, and a bright squeeze of lemon juice, tossed together into a salad that cuts through rich grilled meat like sunshine through clouds.

You will find kachumbari on virtually every table where nyama choma is being served. It provides the perfect counterpoint — cool, crisp, and acidic against the smoky, charred richness of the meat. Every household and restaurant has its own version, but the heart of it never changes: ripe tomatoes, sharp onion, coriander, and citrus. Simple food that asks only for good ingredients and a little patience.

Ingredients

Equipment: Sharp knife, Chopping board, Mixing bowl, Measuring spoons, Citrus juicer (optional)

  • 5 medium ripe tomatoes (600g) — diced
  • 2 medium red onions (250g) — thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander — chopped
  • 1–2 green chillies — finely chopped (optional)
  • 3 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon or lime juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil — optional
  • 1 tsp salt — or to taste
  • ¼ tsp black pepper — optional

Method

  1. Wash all vegetables thoroughly under cold running water.
  2. Dice the tomatoes into neat, bite-sized pieces. Use a sharp knife to keep them clean-edged rather than crushed.
  3. Thinly slice the red onions. If you prefer a milder flavour, soak the sliced onions in cold water for 10 minutes, then drain and pat dry.
  4. Finely chop the coriander and chilli.
  5. Place the tomatoes, onions, coriander, and chilli in a large mixing bowl.
  6. Add the lemon juice, olive oil if using, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Toss gently until everything is evenly combined — do not overmix or the tomatoes will break apart.
  8. Let the salad rest for 10 minutes before serving to allow the flavours to meld beautifully.
  9. Serve chilled or at room temperature alongside nyama choma, ugali, or any grilled dish.

Chef Tips

  • Use ripe but firm tomatoes — overripe tomatoes release too much liquid and make the salad watery.
  • Soaking sliced onions in cold water for 10 minutes removes sharpness while keeping the crunch.
  • Add diced avocado just before serving for a richer, creamier version.
  • Prepare shortly before serving — kachumbari is best when the vegetables are still crisp and fresh.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using overripe tomatoes — they release too much water and the salad becomes soggy.
  • Chopping vegetables unevenly — uniform cuts make the salad look and eat better.
  • Overmixing — causes the tomatoes to break down and lose their texture.
  • Adding salt too far in advance — draws moisture out of the tomatoes and dilutes the dressing.

Cultural Note

Kachumbari is one of Kenya's most recognisable side dishes, served throughout the country and especially beloved at nyama choma joints where it provides a refreshing, bright contrast to grilled meat. It is a dish that requires no cooking, no special equipment, and almost no time — yet it elevates every plate it touches.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best enjoyed fresh. Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 24 hours.

Reheating: Not applicable — serve chilled or at room temperature.

Serve with: Nyama Choma, White Ugali, Chapati, Beef Stew, Grilled Tilapia, Pilau, Githeri, Mishkaki
