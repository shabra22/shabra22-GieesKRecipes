🥚 Kenyan Boiled Egg Curry

Mayai ya Curry ya Kuchemsha

Kenyan Boiled Egg Curry with Swahili coastal spices — hard-boiled eggs fried until golden then simmered in a richly spiced coconut-tomato sauce with cardamom, cinnamon, and fresh coriander. The coastal elevation of the everyday egg curry — fragrant, rich, and deeply satisfying.

This is egg curry elevated with the full Swahili coastal spice palette — whole cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves adding aromatic depth to the tomato base, finished with a generous pour of coconut milk that transforms it into something truly special. The eggs, fried first until golden, develop a slightly crispy exterior that absorbs the coconut sauce beautifully during simmering.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan, Wooden spoon

  • 6 large eggs — hard-boiled and peeled
  • 2 large onions — finely chopped
  • 3 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 200ml thick coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cardamom pods — crushed
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2 whole cloves
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander — chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the peeled hard-boiled eggs and fry for 3—4 minutes, turning, until golden brown all over. Remove and set aside.
  2. In the same pan, add remaining oil. Add whole spices (cardamom, cinnamon, cloves) and fry 30 seconds.
  3. Add onions. Cook until deeply golden — 10 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 1 minute.
  5. Add tomatoes, curry powder, turmeric, and paprika. Cook for 8—10 minutes until thick.
  6. Pour in coconut milk. Stir gently. Bring to a gentle simmer — do not boil hard.
  7. Add the fried eggs. Spoon sauce over them.
  8. Simmer for 8—10 minutes.
  9. Stir in garam masala and lemon juice.
  10. Scatter coriander and serve with rice.

Chef Tips

  • Frying the eggs first creates a golden, lightly crispy exterior that absorbs the coconut curry sauce much better than unfried eggs.
  • The whole spices (cardamom, cinnamon, cloves) at the beginning build the aromatic base of this coastal curry — do not skip them.
  • Never boil the coconut milk vigorously — it splits.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Boiling the coconut milk hard — it separates and becomes oily.
  • Skipping the initial egg frying — the eggs remain smooth and do not absorb the sauce as deeply.

The Science

Frying the egg creates micro-fissures and a porous texture on the white's surface through protein contraction and slight dehydration. This porous exterior absorbs the surrounding sauce by capillary action during simmering — creating a far more flavourful egg than one that was simply boiled and added to sauce.

Cultural Note

This coastal egg curry reflects the Swahili tradition of elevating simple everyday ingredients with aromatic spices and coconut milk — a culinary philosophy that has shaped the most celebrated dishes of the Kenyan coast for centuries.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat very gently on stovetop over low heat.

Serve with: Wali wa Nazi, Basmati Rice, Chapati, Kachumbari, Natural Yogurt
