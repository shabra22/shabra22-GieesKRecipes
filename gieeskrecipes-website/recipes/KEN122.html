🥔 Kenyan Coconut Potatoes

Viazi vya Nazi

Viazi vya Nazi — potatoes simmered in a fragrant coconut milk sauce with onions, tomatoes, turmeric, and fresh coriander. A simple, creamy, and deeply comforting coastal Kenyan side dish that transforms humble potatoes into something beautiful.

Coconut potatoes are one of those dishes that demonstrate what makes Swahili cooking so special — the addition of coconut milk to a simple potato and tomato stew creates something creamy, fragrant, and utterly satisfying. It is a classic coastal side dish served alongside grilled fish, chicken curries, or simply on its own with chapati.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pan with lid, Wooden spoon

  • 600g potatoes — peeled and cut into chunks
  • 400ml thick coconut milk
  • 2 medium onions — finely chopped
  • 3 medium tomatoes — diced
  • 3 garlic cloves — minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger — grated
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander — chopped

Method

  1. Heat oil. Cook onions until soft and golden — 6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes, turmeric, curry powder, and paprika. Cook for 6—8 minutes until thick.
  4. Add potato chunks. Stir to coat in the spiced sauce.
  5. Pour in coconut milk. Stir gently.
  6. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook for 15—20 minutes until potatoes are completely tender.
  7. Do not boil hard — coconut milk splits.
  8. Season and scatter coriander. Serve.

Chef Tips

  • Cut potatoes into even chunks for uniform cooking.
  • Never boil vigorously after adding coconut milk.
  • Slightly mash a few potato pieces to thicken the sauce naturally.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Uneven potato sizes — some raw, some overcooked.
  • Boiling coconut milk hard.

The Science

Potato starch dissolves slightly into the coconut milk during cooking, naturally thickening the sauce without any additional thickeners.

Health Benefits

  • Potatoes provide potassium and vitamin C.
  • Coconut milk adds healthy MCTs.

Cultural Note

Viazi vya Nazi is a beloved everyday dish along Kenya's Swahili coast — the coconut milk elevating simple potatoes into a distinctly coastal preparation that pairs beautifully with the region's grilled fish and seafood.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 3 days.

Reheating: Gently on stovetop with a splash of coconut milk.

Serve with: Grilled Fish, Kuku wa Nazi, Chapati, Kachumbari, Rice
