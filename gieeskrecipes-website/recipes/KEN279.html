🥖 Kenyan Garlic Bread

Mkate wa Vitunguu Saumu

Kenyan Garlic Bread — a whole baguette or ciabatta slathered with garlic-herb butter, wrapped in foil, and baked until the butter has melted throughout, then opened and toasted until golden. The side dish that everyone reaches for first — and always runs out before the meal ends.

Garlic bread appears on every Nairobi restaurant table alongside pasta, soups, salads, and grills. Done properly — with good bread, real butter, generous garlic, and fresh herbs — it is one of the most satisfying accompaniments in the restaurant repertoire.

Ingredients

Equipment: Oven (200°C), Foil, Knife

  • 1 baguette or ciabatta

Garlic-Herb Butter

  • 100g butter — softened
  • 4 garlic cloves — minced to paste
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley — finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh coriander — finely chopped
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Pinch of black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Beat softened butter with garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper until combined.
  3. Slice bread at an angle at 2cm intervals, cutting ¾ of the way through — do not cut all the way through.
  4. Spread garlic butter generously into each cut and over the top.
  5. Wrap tightly in foil.
  6. Bake for 10 minutes.
  7. Open foil. Bake 3—5 more minutes until golden and lightly toasted.
  8. Serve immediately.

Chef Tips

  • Room temperature butter blends with garlic more smoothly than cold.
  • Be generous with the butter — restaurant garlic bread is always more buttery than home versions.
  • The final toasting outside the foil is what creates the crispy top.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Cold butter — does not blend with garlic.
  • Under-buttering — dry garlic bread.
  • Not opening the foil for the final toast.

The Science

The foil creates a steam environment that allows the butter to melt throughout the bread and penetrate the crumb. Opening the foil for the final toasting exposes the buttered surface to direct dry heat, creating Maillard browning on the surface while the interior remains soft and buttery.

Cultural Note

Garlic bread is one of those dishes that transcends its simplicity — at every level of Nairobi's restaurant scene, from the most casual café to the finest hotel, garlic bread arrives and is gone before the main course.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Assembled unbaked garlic bread freezes for 2 months.

Reheating: Bake directly from frozen — add 5 minutes to time.
