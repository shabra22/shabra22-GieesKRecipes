☕ Kenyan Café Tiramisu

Tiramisu ya Kahawa ya Kenya

Kenyan Café Tiramisu — the Italian classic made with Kenya AA single-origin espresso, layered between mascarpone cream and cocoa-dusted sponge fingers. The Kenyan version is more intensely coffee-flavoured than any other — because the coffee is extraordinary.

Tiramisu made with Kenya AA espresso is a different dessert from the standard Italian version. The fruit-forward brightness, the black currant notes, and the extraordinary depth of Kenyan single-origin coffee transform every layer of this classic. Nairobi's finest cafés have made this dessert their own.

Ingredients

Equipment: Mixing bowls, Electric beater, Deep dish (20x30cm)

  • 300g sponge fingers (savoiardi)

Espresso Soak

  • 2 cups strong Kenyan espresso — cooled
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Mascarpone Cream

  • 500g mascarpone
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 120g icing sugar
  • 300ml whipping cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Dusting

  • Cocoa powder
  • Dark chocolate shavings

Method

  1. Mix cooled espresso and sugar in a shallow bowl.
  2. Beat egg yolks and icing sugar until pale and thick.
  3. Fold in mascarpone and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Whip cream to soft peaks. Fold into mascarpone mixture.
  5. Dip each sponge finger briefly in coffee — 2 seconds each side. Do not soak.
  6. Layer: dipped sponge fingers, mascarpone cream, sponge fingers, mascarpone cream.
  7. Dust top generously with cocoa powder.
  8. Refrigerate at least 4 hours — overnight is ideal.
  9. Add chocolate shavings before serving.

Chef Tips

  • Brief dipping only — over-soaked sponge fingers become a soggy puddle.
  • Kenyan espresso is the star — use the best single-origin available.
  • The tiramisu improves significantly overnight — make a day ahead.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-soaking the sponge fingers.
  • Serving immediately — needs time to set.

The Science

The sponge fingers absorb the espresso through capillary action. A 2-second dip allows sufficient penetration without structural failure. The mascarpone cream sets during refrigeration as the cold consolidates the air bubbles incorporated during whipping, creating the characteristic firm but yielding texture.

Cultural Note

Kenyan café tiramisu is a statement about Kenya's coffee heritage — when the world's finest coffee is used as the soaking liquid, the result is a tiramisu that Italian visitors recognise as fundamentally the same dish but dramatically more intense.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Serve cold.

Serve with: Kenyan Iced Coffee (KEN249), As a standalone dessert
