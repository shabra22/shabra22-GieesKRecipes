🍫 Kenyan Brownies

Brouni za Kenya

Kenyan Brownies — rich, fudgy dark chocolate brownies made with Kenyan coffee for extra depth, with a crinkly top and gooey centre. The perfect café counter brownie — intense, slightly bittersweet, and impossible to eat just one of.

The perfect brownie has been perfected at Nairobi's best cafés — fudgy rather than cakey, with that characteristic crinkly sugary top. The addition of Kenyan espresso amplifies the chocolate flavour without tasting of coffee.

Ingredients

Equipment: 20x20cm square baking tin, Bain-marie or microwave, Oven (170°C)

  • 200g dark chocolate (70%) — chopped
  • 150g butter
  • 200g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp strong Kenyan espresso
  • 80g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 170°C. Grease and line tin.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together. Cool slightly.
  3. Whisk sugar into chocolate mixture.
  4. Add eggs one at a time — whisk vigorously after each.
  5. Add vanilla and espresso.
  6. Fold in flour, cocoa, and salt. Do not over-mix.
  7. Pour into tin. Bake 22—25 minutes.
  8. The top should be set and crinkly; a skewer should come out with a few moist crumbs.
  9. Cool completely before cutting — the fudgy centre sets as it cools.

Chef Tips

  • Under-bake slightly — fudgy brownies are the goal, not cakey.
  • Cool completely before cutting — cutting warm brownies produces crumbles.
  • The crinkly top comes from the vigorous whisking of eggs and sugar.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-baking — cakey instead of fudgy.
  • Cutting hot brownies.

The Science

The crinkly top forms when sugar and egg foam rises to the surface during baking and sets as a meringue-like crust. The fudgy interior is achieved by a higher ratio of fat (chocolate and butter) to flour — the fat prevents the starch and protein networks from setting fully, keeping the centre soft.

Cultural Note

Nairobi's café brownie culture has reached international standards — the best brownies in the city rival any served in London or New York, partly because of the extraordinary Kenyan coffee used in the batter.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Room temperature 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Warm briefly in oven.

Serve with: Vanilla ice cream, Kenyan Iced Coffee (KEN249), As a café counter snack
