🫘 Cambuulo

Cambuulo

A comforting Somali dish of well-cooked azuki beans mashed with butter and sugar, its gentle sweetness making it as much a treat as a filling everyday meal.

Cambuulo's simple combination of slow-cooked beans, ghee, and sugar might sound unusual to outside ears, but the result is a genuinely comforting, mildly sweet dish beloved across Somalia, often eaten for a light dinner or as a satisfying snack.

Ingredients

Equipment: Large pot

  • 300 g (1 1/2 cups) small red or azuki beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 L (4 1/4 cups) water
  • 80 g (1/3 cup) ghee or butter
  • 3-4 tbsp sugar, or to taste
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Drain the soaked beans and place in a large pot with the water.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 45-60 minutes, until the beans are completely tender and starting to break down.
  3. Drain any excess liquid, leaving the beans slightly moist.
  4. Mash the beans lightly with a spoon, leaving some texture.
  5. Stir in the ghee, sugar, and a pinch of salt until well combined.
  6. Serve warm, with an extra small pat of ghee on top if desired.

Chef Tips

  • Cook the beans until very tender - they should be soft enough to mash easily, not just fork-tender.
  • Adjust the sugar to taste; this dish is meant to have a gentle, comforting sweetness rather than being overly sweet.
  • A generous final pat of ghee on top is traditional and adds richness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Undercooking the beans, leaving them too firm to mash properly.
  • Being too sparing with the ghee, which is essential to the dish comforting richness.
  • Over-mashing into a completely smooth paste, losing the pleasant, slightly chunky texture.

Cultural Note

Cambuulo is a beloved comfort dish across Somalia, its simple sweetness making it popular with children and often served for a light evening meal.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating: Reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of water, or in the microwave.

Serve with: Canjeero, A glass of milk
