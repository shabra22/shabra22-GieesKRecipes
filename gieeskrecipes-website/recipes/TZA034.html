🍖 Tanzanian Beef Bone Soup

Supu ya Mifupa

Supu ya Mifupa — Tanzanian bone broth soup. Large beef or goat bones slow-simmered for 2 hours with vegetables, ginger, and spices into a rich, deeply nourishing, golden broth. Tanzania's most restorative food — given to the sick, nursing mothers, and anyone who needs strength.

Supu ya Mifupa is Tanzania's healing food — the soup given to the sick, the recovering, and new mothers. The 2-hour simmer extracts collagen, marrow, and minerals from the bones into a broth of extraordinary nutritional depth. Sold at roadside stalls in large pots, it is the working person's nourishment.

Ingredients

Equipment: Very large pot

  • 1 kg beef or goat bones — with marrow
  • 2 medium onions — halved
  • 3 medium carrots — roughly chopped
  • 2 celery stalks — optional
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp ginger — sliced
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 3 litres water
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander
  • Fresh lemon to serve

Method

  1. Blanch bones in boiling water for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse.
  2. Place bones in a large pot with all vegetables, garlic, ginger, salt, and peppercorns.
  3. Cover with 3 litres cold water.
  4. Bring to a boil. Skim foam.
  5. Reduce to the lowest simmer. Cover and cook for 2 hours.
  6. Strain. Return broth to pot.
  7. Taste and adjust salt.
  8. Serve garnished with coriander and a wedge of lemon.

Chef Tips

  • Blanching first removes blood and impurities — clearer broth.
  • The lowest possible simmer produces the clearest, most elegant broth.
  • Lemon squeezed at the table brightens the whole bowl.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Hard boiling — produces cloudy broth.
  • Skipping blanching.

The Science

Long simmering dissolves collagen from bones and connective tissue into gelatin — producing a broth that sets to a jelly when chilled. This gelatin is what makes bone broth uniquely nourishing and soothing to the digestive system.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 4 days. Freeze 3 months.

Reheating: Heat to boiling before serving.

Serve with: As a starter, With Chapati (TZA004), With Ugali (TZA001), With fresh lemon
