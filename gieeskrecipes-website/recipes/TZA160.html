🫓 Zanzibar Stone Town Flatbread

Mkate wa Ufuta

Zanzibar Mkate wa Ufuta — a soft, slightly sweet flatbread crusted with sesame seeds on one side, pan-fried until golden. Zanzibar Stone Town's morning bread — softer than chapati, thicker than injera, and unmistakably sesame-fragrant.

Mkate wa Ufuta is the Stone Town morning flatbread — distinct from chapati by its thickness, its slight sweetness, and most importantly its sesame seed crust. It is the bread that vendors sell from baskets in the narrow lanes of Stone Town each morning.

Ingredients

Equipment: Rolling pin, Wide pan

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 200ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 4 tbsp sesame seeds — toasted

Method

  1. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. Add oil and warm water. Mix to a soft dough.
  3. Knead 5 minutes. Rest covered 60 minutes.
  4. Divide into 8 balls. Roll each into a round about 8mm thick.
  5. Press one side firmly into sesame seeds.
  6. Cook sesame-side down in a dry pan over medium heat for 4 minutes.
  7. Flip. Cook other side 3 minutes.
  8. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • The sesame crust goes down first — maximum contact time with the pan.
  • Thicker than chapati — allow enough time.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Too thin — loses its characteristic thickness.
  • Too high heat — seeds burn.

The Science

Sesame seeds pressed firmly into the dough surface become embedded as the dough cooks — the starch on the dough surface gelatinises and bonds with the sesame protein, effectively gluing the seeds in place.

Health Benefits

  • Sesame seeds add calcium and zinc.

Cultural Note

Mkate wa Ufuta is the bread of Zanzibar's mornings — sold from baskets by women in the narrow lanes of Stone Town, bought by commuters and students before the day begins. Its sesame fragrance is the first smell of a Stone Town morning.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Store wrapped 2 days.

Reheating: Warm in dry pan.

Serve with: Chai ya Viungo (TZA144), Mbaazi wa Nazi (TZA131), Honey
