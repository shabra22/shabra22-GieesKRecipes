🥚 Tanzanian Egg Rolls

Mkeka wa Mayai

Tanzanian Egg Rolls — a chapati spread with spiced minced beef, vegetables, and beaten egg, rolled up and pan-fried until golden and sealed. The Tanzanian street roll — hearty, fast, and completely portable. Every ingredient wrapped into one convenient cylinder.

Tanzanian egg rolls are not the Chinese egg roll — they are chapati rolls filled with egg and spiced meat, pan-fried to seal. They are the ultimate portable street food: everything you need in one hand-held roll.

Ingredients

Equipment: Wide flat pan

  • 4 chapatis
  • 4 eggs — beaten and seasoned
  • 200g minced beef — cooked and spiced
  • 1 onion — thinly sliced
  • 1 tomato — finely diced
  • 1 green chilli — sliced
  • 2 tbsp coriander
  • ½ tsp curry powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil for frying

Method

  1. Season and cook minced beef with curry powder, salt, onion. Cool slightly.
  2. Heat pan over medium heat. Brush with oil.
  3. Place a chapati in the pan.
  4. Pour 1 beaten egg over the chapati — spread with the back of a spoon.
  5. Allow egg to set 1 minute.
  6. Spoon 2 tbsp spiced beef, tomato, chilli, and coriander across the centre.
  7. Roll the chapati up tightly.
  8. Pan-fry on all sides until golden and sealed — 2—3 minutes.
  9. Cut diagonally. Serve with pili pili.

Chef Tips

  • Work quickly — the egg sets fast.
  • Do not overfill — rolling becomes impossible.
  • Press the seam of the roll down in the pan first — it seals in 30 seconds.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overfilling.
  • Moving too slowly — egg fully sets before rolling.

The Science

The egg layer between the chapati and filling serves as a binding agent — the proteins set around the filling, gluing the chapati to the contents during the final pan-fry. This is why the roll stays together without any additional adhesive.

Cultural Note

Tanzanian egg rolls are the street food of efficiency — everything the body needs wrapped up and portable, made in minutes, eaten on the go. They are the breakfast-lunch of Dar es Salaam's working people.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Pan-fry briefly.
