🍌 Tanzanian Banana Fritters

Ndizi za Kukaanga

Tanzanian Ndizi za Kukaanga — ripe bananas dipped in a light, slightly sweetened batter and deep-fried until golden and crispy outside, melting and sweet within. The evening street dessert fritter — warm, golden, and irresistible.

Banana fritters are the sweet street food of Tanzanian evenings — the ripe bananas coated in a simple batter and fried until the outside is golden and crispy while the banana inside caramelises in its own sweetness. Simple, affordable, and completely satisfying.

Ingredients

Equipment: Deep frying pan

  • 4 ripe bananas — halved lengthways

Batter

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • Pinch of salt
  • 200ml water
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Honey or powdered sugar to serve

Method

  1. Make batter: whisk flour, sugar, cardamom, salt and water until smooth.
  2. Halve bananas lengthways.
  3. Heat oil to 175°C.
  4. Dip banana halves in batter — coat completely.
  5. Fry 3—4 minutes until golden on all sides.
  6. Drain on paper towels.
  7. Drizzle with honey or dust with powdered sugar.
  8. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Very ripe bananas — sweeter, more flavourful.
  • The batter should be smooth but not too thick.
  • Serve immediately — they lose crispiness as they cool.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Under-ripe bananas — starchy, not sweet.
  • Too thick batter — doughy.

Cultural Note

Banana fritters are the sweet ending to a Tanzanian evening — bought from a vendor as the night market winds down, eaten warm from the paper bag, the cardamom and caramelised banana the last taste of the day.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Best eaten immediately.

Reheating: Briefly in hot oil.

Serve with: Honey, Powdered Sugar, As a standalone dessert
