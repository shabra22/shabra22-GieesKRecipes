🍬 Tanzanian Tamarind Candy

Pipi ya Ukwaju

Tanzanian Pipi ya Ukwaju — tamarind pulp cooked with sugar and chilli, rolled into small balls and coated in sugar. The sour-sweet-spicy coastal candy — one of Zanzibar's most distinctive confections, with a flavour profile that goes from sweet to sour to mildly spicy in a single bite.

Tamarind candy is one of the most distinctive Tanzanian sweets — the complexity of the tamarind (sour, slightly bitter, deeply fruity) combined with sugar and a touch of chilli produces a candy that surprises with every bite. It is sold at every coastal market in small bags.

Ingredients

Equipment: Heavy saucepan, Greased surface or parchment

  • 200g block tamarind — soaked in 1 cup warm water and strained
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • Pinch of salt

Coating: ½ cup sugar mixed with ¼ tsp chilli powder

    Method

    1. Combine strained tamarind pulp, sugar, chilli, ginger, and salt in a saucepan.
    2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 15—18 minutes.
    3. The mixture will thicken and begin to pull away from the sides.
    4. Test: drop a small amount in cold water — it should form a soft ball.
    5. Remove from heat. Cool until handleable but still warm — 10 minutes.
    6. With greased hands, roll into small balls (1.5cm).
    7. Roll each ball in the chilli-sugar coating.
    8. Place on parchment. Cool completely.

    Chef Tips

    • The mixture is very sticky — grease your hands generously.
    • Work quickly — the candy firms as it cools.
    • The chilli coating is part of the experience — do not skip.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Under-cooking — too sticky to roll.
    • Letting it cool too much before rolling — too hard to shape.

    The Science

    Tamarind contains tartaric acid, which makes the sugar inversion reaction more complete during cooking — producing a softer, more hygroscopic candy that absorbs moisture from the air (preventing crystallisation). This is why tamarind candy stays soft and chewy rather than becoming hard and brittle like plain boiled sugar candy.

    Cultural Note

    Tamarind candy is Zanzibar's most polarising sweet — those who love it cannot stop eating it; those who encounter it unprepared are surprised by its intensity. The combination of sour, sweet, and spicy in a single small candy is the Swahili coast's flavour philosophy in miniature.

    Storage & Serving

    Storage: Airtight container 3 weeks.

    Reheating: Not applicable.
