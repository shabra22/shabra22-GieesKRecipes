🥜 Makonde Peanut Porridge

Uji wa Karanga — Wamakonde

Makonde Peanut Porridge — cassava or maize flour porridge enriched with freshly ground roasted peanuts and sweetened with honey. The high-protein breakfast of southern Tanzania — the Makonde peanut enrichment of the morning porridge makes it one of the most sustaining breakfasts in the country.

Makonde peanut porridge is the breakfast that sustains southern Tanzania's great carvers through long hours of work — the combination of cassava starch and groundnut protein provides energy that lasts well into the afternoon.

Ingredients

Equipment: Saucepan, Whisk

  • 1 cup cassava flour or maize flour
  • 3 tbsp freshly roasted and ground groundnuts
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Whisk flour with 1 cup cold water until smooth.
  2. Bring remaining 3 cups water to a simmer.
  3. Pour flour mixture in slowly, whisking constantly.
  4. Cook 12—15 minutes, stirring, until thick.
  5. Stir in ground roasted peanuts.
  6. Remove from heat. Add honey and salt.
  7. Serve warm.

Chef Tips

  • Freshly roasted peanuts produce far more flavour than commercial peanut butter.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using commercial peanut butter.

The Science

See TZA246 for porridge cooking science. The addition of groundnut protein to a starch-based porridge creates a more complete amino acid profile — the lysine from groundnuts complements the methionine in the cassava flour.

Cultural Note

Makonde peanut porridge is the breakfast of the carvers — sustaining the remarkable artistic tradition of the Makonde people through long hours of intricate work. The food and the art are connected: both require patience, both require local resources, both define the Makonde.

Storage & Serving

Storage: Refrigerate 2 days.

Reheating: Gently with added water.

Serve with: As a standalone breakfast
